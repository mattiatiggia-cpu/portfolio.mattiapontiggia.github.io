Based on the writings in Nick Land’s writings collection ‘Meltdown’, Collasso recounts a journey into the realm of the image, questioning the value given to the symbol through machinic thinking and recalculating beauty by technologically accelerating its symbolic value in a continuous recalculation.

The body is no longer the active ingredient in the formula for the creation of aesthetics: covered in odourless plastic, the veil of maya settles over buildings and streets.
