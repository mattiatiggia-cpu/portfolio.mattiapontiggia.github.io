A Blank Space is a strategic and radical initiative taking place at MUDEC (Milan Museum of Cultures) in 2025. The project stems from a desire to transform visitors from passive observers into active co-creators, offering a space for free expression within a traditionally regimented institution.

Vision \ Mission

The space is a completely white room, furnished with minimalist elements (sofas, tables, soft surfaces) that are also white. Here, every surface becomes a canvas: visitors can draw, write and share their thoughts using washable markers. The main aim is to allow for the immediate expression of thoughts, emotions, critiques or ‘immediate impressions’ following the visit, giving a voice to those who do not feel represented by the official narratives.

Rules \ Innovation

The project is founded on four pillars:

  • Total expression: You can write anywhere.
  • Absence of hierarchy: Every content, from children's drawings to theoretical texts, has equal dignity.
  • Collective responsibility: Respect for the expression of others.
  • Impermanence: The traces are temporary; they are documented and archived daily before being erased to make room for new contributions.

Experiences \ Community

Beyond the visual component, Blank Space hosts listening parties and immersive audio experiences that redefine the relationship between body and space. The heart of the community is the BLANK Card, a symbolic-priced card (10€/year) that offers discounts, special access for collectives and marginalized realities, and the right to participate in assemblies to co-curate the rules of the space.
