Born out of the “MADEBACK” workshop at the Politecnico di Milano, The Last Day on Earth tells the story of the end of a teenage romance, the moment when the simplest gestures become rituals of farewell. Through the reworking of sweatshirts and garments associated with youth pop culture, the project creates a ritualistic corset dress.
