Pilgrimages explores the complex relationship between humanity and the natural world. Inspired by Giacomo Leopardi’s “Dialogue between Nature and an Icelander”, the project delves into nature’s indifference and humanity’s quest for harmony and coexistence. Translating this philosophical concept into tangible garments,
Drawing strong inspiration from the Gorpcore aesthetic, the collection features highly functional and utilitarian elements such as transformable silhouettes, integrated rucksacks, strategic mesh inserts to ensure breathability, and protective linings. Through a process of deconstruction and layering, traditional and historic outdoor wear has been completely reimagined, including oversized expedition jackets, asymmetrical cargo trousers, tactical vests and hooded capes.
Fabric research focused on technical performance and material contrast, using wool, nylon and protective treatments to simulate textures such as frost and dirt;
Thanks to Olmetex for supplying all the necessary materials.
